What is a Diet? — Short Paragraph

A diet is the collection of foods and drinks a person regularly consumes. A healthy diet supplies the body with energy, building blocks for repair, and nutrients to support all bodily processes. The goal is to balance macronutrients (carbs, proteins, fats) with sufficient vitamins, minerals, fiber and water.

Common Types of Diets

Balanced Diet

All food groups in moderate portions — best for general health.

Mediterranean

Fruits, vegetables, fish, olive oil — heart friendly.

Low-Carb / Keto

Low carbs, high fat — used for rapid weight loss short-term.

Vegan

No animal products — plant based for ethics/health.

Intermittent Fasting

Eating within windows (e.g., 16:8) — supports weight control & metabolic flexibility.

Vegetarian

No meat, may include eggs/dairy — balanced plant focus.

Practical Tips (Short Points)

Plan meals

Preparing plates ahead avoids impulsive choices.

Choose whole foods

Whole grains, legumes, nuts & fresh produce over processed options.

Watch portions

Use smaller plates; stop when ~80% full.

Protein first

Include quality protein in each meal to support satiety and repair.

Key Components (More Detail)

  • Carbohydrates: Primary energy — prefer whole grains, fruits, legumes.
  • Proteins: Repair & enzymes — eggs, dairy, fish, pulses, tofu.
  • Fats: Essential fatty acids — olive oil, nuts, seeds, fatty fish.
  • Vitamins & Minerals: From colourful fruits & vegetables — aim for a rainbow daily.
  • Fiber & Water: Support digestion; aim for 25–35g fiber and ~2–3 L water depending on needs.

Common Unhealthy Habits to Avoid

Skipping meals, relying on sugary drinks, excessive processed foods, very low calorie dieting without supervision — all can harm energy levels and long-term health.
